# K9 Privacy Policy (Web Version)
_Effective November 2, 2026_

K9 is an app for training blocks, nutrition and coaching clients. This policy covers the K9 web app that runs in your browser. The K9 iPhone app has its own policy: it has no accounts, and its data stays on your phone.

## In short
- You can use K9 without an account — then everything stays only in this browser.
- If you sign in, your data is also stored in the cloud: it opens on your other devices and for a coach you connected yourself.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Without an account
Blocks, sets, your profile, weigh-ins, nutrition and clients are stored in this browser’s storage. We don’t receive them. If you clear the site’s data in your browser, they will be erased — export a copy now and then.

## With an account
K9 uses Google Firebase services for sign-in and storage:
- **Firebase Authentication** stores your email and sign-in method, and if you sign in with Google, also your Google profile name and photo (K9 doesn’t use them). Your password is stored in protected form: neither we nor your coach can see it.
- **Cloud Firestore** stores your K9 data: blocks, sets, notes, your profile (sex, birth year, height, activity, protein and fat settings), weigh-ins, food log, custom exercises and foods, settings, and in coach mode — clients and notes about them. The database is located in the European Union (Warsaw).

Google processes this data as a service provider under the [Firebase terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Database rules restrict access: only you and the coaches you connect can see your data.

## Coaches and clients
- A coach creates an invite code, and the client enters it in their app. After that, the coach sees the client’s blocks, workouts, profile, weight and nutrition and can edit them, and also sees the name the client chose for them. The coach doesn’t see the client’s settings or the client’s own clients.
- Either side can disconnect at any time: access is closed right away, and the client’s data is removed from the coach’s app.
- Coaches: if you track clients without an account and enter their data yourself, get their consent to store it.

## Bug reports
The “Report a Bug” button sends us your text and, if you provide it, an email for a reply. If “Attach technical details” is on, the report also includes the app version, browser, screen size, language, current screen, sync status, recent page errors and your account ID. Workouts, nutrition and weight are not included. Reports are stored in the same Firebase database and are used only to fix bugs; we will delete them on request.

## Files
- “Export All Data” creates JSON and CSV files with your data in your browser’s downloads.
- “Send Report to Coach” and “Send Program” create files that you share yourself. From there, the service you choose handles the file under its own terms.

## Age
Calorie tracking is not intended for people under 18: if your profile shows such an age, calorie targets are not displayed.

## How to delete your data
- “Delete All Data” in your profile erases the data in this browser and, if you’re signed in, in your account on all devices.
- “Delete Account” deletes your account and all its cloud data, along with connections to coaches and clients.
- “Sign Out” removes the data from this browser; it stays in your account.
- Files you have already exported or sent remain wherever you saved or sent them.

## Your rights
You can view, export or delete your data in the app at any time. If you have a question about your data, write to us — contact details are below.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app.

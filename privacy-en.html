K9 Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

K9 is an app for training blocks, nutrition and coaching clients. It is built so that your data stays with you.

In short

  • The app has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything you enter is stored only on your device.
  • Data leaves your device only when you send a file yourself or allow K9 to exchange data with Apple Health.

What is stored on your device

  • Training blocks, sets, weights and notes.
  • Your profile: sex, year of birth, height, activity level and protein and fat settings.
  • Weigh-ins and your food diary.
  • In coach mode, client data that you enter yourself or import from their reports: name, goal, notes, profile (sex, year of birth, height, activity level, protein and fat settings), workouts, body weight and nutrition.

We do not receive this data: it is not sent to our servers and is not synced through iCloud. Like other apps’ data, it is included in your iPhone backup if you have backups turned on (in iCloud or on a computer).

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, K9 reads your body weight, steps and active energy with your permission, and writes the body weight you enter in the app. This data is used only inside the app: it is not shared with third parties and is not used for advertising. You can revoke access in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → K9.

When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to:

  • "Export All Data" creates JSON and CSV files with your own data (blocks, profile, weigh-ins, including those read from Health, and nutrition, plus the exercise and food catalogs) and opens the share sheet.
  • "Send Report to Coach" creates a file with your name, profile, training blocks, weigh-ins and nutrition for the period you choose. "Send Program" to a client creates a file with the block plan: exercises, schemes, planned weights and notes. You send the file yourself via AirDrop, a messenger or email.

From then on, the service you choose handles the file under its own terms.

For coaches

If you manage clients in K9, get their consent to store and process their training, body weight and nutrition data.

Age

Calorie tracking is not intended for people under 18: if the profile shows such an age, calorie targets are hidden.

Deleting your data

  • Profile & Goals → "Delete All Data" erases everything from the device.
  • Deleting the app also deletes all of its data.
  • Entries that K9 added to Apple Health are deleted in the Health app.
  • Files you have already exported or sent stay wherever you saved or sent them.

Your rights

Only you hold your data: you can view, export or delete it in the app at any time. If you have a question about your data, contact us using the details below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on the K9 App Store page.

FAQ

How do I change the language? Profile & Goals → Language. Choose Ukrainian, English, Russian or the system language.

Why sign in? With an account, your workouts, nutrition and weight are stored in the cloud: they are on all your devices and in the K9 web app, and a coach you connected sees them right away. Without an account everything works as before — only on this iPhone. To sign in: Profile & Goals → Account.

How do I connect a coach? Ask your coach for an invite code, then go to Profile & Goals → Coach Online → Connect a Coach and enter the code. You need an account.

How do I turn on coach mode? Profile & Goals → "I'm a Coach". The Schedule, Clients, Payments and My Training tabs appear at the bottom.

How do I move my data to another iPhone? Sign in to the same account on the new iPhone — your data loads from the cloud. Without an account, K9 data moves with your iPhone when you transfer to a new device or restore from a backup (iCloud or a computer). "Export All Data" creates a JSON and CSV copy for spreadsheets and other apps; it can’t be imported back into K9.

How do I delete my account? Profile & Goals → Account → "Delete Account". The account and all its cloud data are deleted; data on your iPhone stays.

How do I delete all my data? Profile & Goals → "Delete All Data". If you are signed in, the data is also deleted from your account on all devices. Deleting the app deletes its data on the device.

Why don't I see calorie targets? Fill in your profile: sex, year of birth, height and weight. Targets are hidden for profiles under 18.

Privacy

Without an account your data stays on your device; with an account it is also stored in the Google Firebase cloud. Read the privacy policy.

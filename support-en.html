FAQ

How do I change the language? Profile & Goals → Language. Choose Ukrainian, English, Russian or the system language.

How do I turn on coach mode? Profile & Goals → "I'm a Coach". A Clients tab appears at the bottom.

How do I move my data to another iPhone? K9 data moves with your iPhone when you transfer to a new device or restore from a backup (iCloud or a computer). "Export All Data" creates a JSON and CSV copy for spreadsheets and other apps; it can’t be imported back into K9.

How do I delete all my data? Profile & Goals → "Delete All Data". Deleting the app also deletes all of its data.

Why don't I see calorie targets? Fill in your profile: sex, year of birth, height and weight. Targets are hidden for profiles under 18.
